# EZStore Risk Assessment

## High Risk
- Security gaps in auth, auditing, and environment handling.
- Incomplete production operations and deployment pipeline.
- lack of scalability controls for catalog growth and traffic spikes.

## Medium Risk
- Admin and customer workflows are incomplete for full ecommerce operations.
- Current data layer needs stronger indexing and migration discipline.

## Low Risk
- Basic app structure is in place and extendable.
- Frontend and backend frameworks are modern and suitable for production growth.

## Overall Risk Level
Medium to High until the backend foundation, security hardening, and monitoring layer are completed.
